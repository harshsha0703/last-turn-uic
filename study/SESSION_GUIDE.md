# Functional evaluation
Recruit nine appropriate participants, three per persona. Obtain consent before recording. Use anonymous IDs; collect required demographics separately. Rehearse and pilot first.

Task 1: Navigate from SCE main entrance to Inner Circle using the team-checked route.
Task 2: Use the CDRLC building reference to identify the entrance and locate the stairs or elevator; check posted signs in person. This is a landmark task, not a verified classroom route.

Ask what participants are trying to do, what information they use, where they become uncertain, how they recover, and how they confirm their destination. Record actual actions, exact statements and difficulties. Capture approved video and anonymized screenshots. Export events after each task; these are supplemental, not proof of physical arrival. Keep recordings private. Iterate between sessions and document actual changes. Code at least four themes with two real quotes each.
